# NotRover

NotRover is a software company. **RoverTools** is our line of software built for
the public - open source and not.

## Orange Copy Paste

The first app in the RoverTools series. A clipboard that remembers: everything
you copy - text, images, files - kept in a searchable history next to your
notes, on every device. End-to-end encrypted, and usable offline.

- Searchable history, pins, and groups
- Notes, alongside your clipboard
- Optional real-time sync across your devices
- End-to-end encrypted - the servers never see your content
- Works offline. No account needed to start.
